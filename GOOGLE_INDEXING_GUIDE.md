# 🔍 Guide: Indexing Your Web App on Google Search

To make your web app appear on **Google Search** when anyone searches for terms like *"AI Weather Intelligence"*, *"ML Weather Checker Aadii0916"*, or *"Real-Time Weather AI Forecast"*, follow these steps:

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Submit Your URL to Google Search Console (100% Free)

Google automatically crawls public websites, but you can request instant indexing using Google Search Console:

1. Go to **[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)**.
2. Sign in with your Google Account.
3. Click **Add Property** or paste your live URL into the top search bar:
   `https://ml-weather-checker-fzsj9k3wagw3cu4vcw69nd.streamlit.app`
4. Click **Request Indexing**.
5. Google will crawl your app and list it on Google Search within a few days!

## 🔗 Step 3: Share Your Link on Social Media & GitHub

Google ranks websites higher based on "backlinks" (links pointing to your site):
1. Add your live URL (`https://ml-weather-checker-fzsj9k3wagw3cu4vcw69nd.streamlit.app`) to your **GitHub Repository About Section** ([github.com/Aadii0916/ml-weather-checker](https://github.com/Aadii0916/ml-weather-checker)).
2. Share the link on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Reddit.
3. Once Google sees links pointing to your site, it indexes and ranks your web app much faster!
